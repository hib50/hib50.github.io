IN DEVELOPMENT • DAYLIGHT HARVESTING • VALIDATION

Automated Daylight Harvesting Validation Platform

A controlled test system being developed to evaluate how EcoBright’s LED 2.0 fixture reduces artificial-light demand as ambient daylight increases, while maintaining stable work-plane illuminance.

STATUS: IN PROGRESS
ESP32 VEML7700 Python I²C 0–10 V Dimming Lux Validation

RESEARCH QUESTION

Does the fixture reduce power as daylight increases while preserving useful illuminance?

The reduced-scope study compares a standard LED baseline against the EcoBright LED 2.0 system across four controlled daylight-access levels: no daylight, low daylight, medium daylight and high daylight.

TEST ARCHITECTURE

Controlled daylight emulation

A secondary LED panel acts as the daylight-emulation source. Diffusion and matte reflective surfaces are used to produce broad ambient illumination instead of a concentrated hotspot. The test area is isolated from external light using black curtains and a fixed geometry.

EXPERIMENTAL MATRIX

Four standardized daylight conditions

No Daylight

Control condition with the emulating source disabled and the fixture at full demand.

Low Daylight

Weak diffuse ambient contribution used to capture the lower-bound harvesting response.

Medium Daylight

Moderate diffuse ambient contribution representing a typical operating state.

High Daylight

Strong ambient contribution used to evaluate upper-bound dimming behavior.

INSTRUMENTATION

What the system measures

Ambient / Work-Plane Sensing VEML7700 digital lux sensors + calibrated handheld lux meters
Dimming Signal 0–10 V analog control line monitored through safe voltage-divider instrumentation
Power Verification Discrete direct measurements at key operating thresholds
Data Output Timed lux, control voltage and mapped wattage estimates
Commercial Scaling Energy, cost and carbon outputs per fixture and per 100 fixtures

MODELING STRATEGY

Turning dimming voltage into commercial performance

The proposal models instantaneous dimming ratio from measured control voltage relative to the 10 V reference, then maps that ratio to fixture power.

Accumulated energy is computed over the test interval and compared against the standard-LED baseline. Direct power spot-checks at major operating points are planned to validate the mathematical mapping.
