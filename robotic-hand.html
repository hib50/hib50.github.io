RESEARCH DIRECTION

Learning a robot's physical configuration

This early-stage research explores the development of a multi-jointed robotic hand that can identify its joint configuration and adapt its physical structure. Environmental perception, embodied AI, mechanical reconfiguration and possible future fault detection are longer-term research directions. The project remains under development.
