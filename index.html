ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Hassan.

I'm a third-year Mechatronics Engineering student at Toronto Metropolitan University with a strong passion for robotics, autonomous systems, and embedded technologies. Engineering is more than just my field of study; it's something I genuinely enjoy pursuing through research, personal projects, and hands-on experimentation.

As an undergraduate researcher at TMU's ELIXIR Lab, I work with robotic and electromechanical systems, gaining experience in hardware troubleshooting, schematic interpretation, embedded electronics, and autonomous robotics. My work exposes me to technologies such as ROS 2, Linux, robotic simulation, and the broader research areas of embodied AI, reinforcement learning, and intelligent robotic control.

Alongside my research, I work as a Test and Validation Engineering Intern at EcoBright Energy, where I conduct experimental testing, analyze system performance, and validate energy-efficient lighting and automated daylight-harvesting technologies.

What interests me most is the challenge of developing intelligent systems that can interact with their environments. Whether I'm troubleshooting hardware, exploring a new robotics concept, or working through a complex technical problem, I enjoy the process of turning ideas into functioning systems.
