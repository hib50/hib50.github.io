RP2040 Multi-Sensor Embedded Board

A custom embedded-system schematic centered around the RP2040, combining inertial sensing, environmental sensing, external flash storage, USB-C connectivity, dual-voltage regulation and support circuitry.

POWER ARCHITECTURE

Separate rails for digital logic and sensor requirements

The schematic derives a 3.3 V rail from the USB-C / 5 V input and then creates a separate 1.8 V rail for the ICM-20948. Local decoupling capacitors are distributed around the controller and sensors to support stable operation.

COMMUNICATION

Digital buses and board support

QSPI Flash

The W25Q16 flash connects through RP2040 QSPI signals for external program/data storage.

Sensor Bus

The IMU and environmental sensor are integrated through shared digital sensor lines with appropriate pull-up support.

USB-C

USB differential lines are routed to the RP2040, with CC resistors and power entry included in the schematic.

Debug / Reset

The design includes SWD support, reset circuitry and a BOOTSEL connection to the flash path.

DESIGN TAKEAWAYS

What this project demonstrates

  • Breaking an embedded system into functional blocks before detailed routing.
  • Managing mixed-voltage requirements across sensors and MCU support circuitry.
  • Integrating USB, flash, sensor interfaces and reset/debug support around a bare microcontroller.
  • Applying decoupling and pull-up networks at the schematic level.
